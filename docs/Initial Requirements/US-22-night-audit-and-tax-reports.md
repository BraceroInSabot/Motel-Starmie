[US-22] Night Audit, Tax Summary and Locked Fee Reports

# User Story

As a General Manager, I want Night Audit reports, tax summaries and a Locked Fee report, So that I can monitor financial results, meet tax obligations and release locked funds when necessary.

# Acceptance Criteria

* Scenario 1: Night Audit report
   * Given the Night Audit ran for a business day
   * When I open the Night Audit report for that day
   * Then the system displays the charges and taxes posted in that run
* Scenario 2: Tax summary
   * Given there are taxes posted in a period
   * When I generate the tax summary for that period
   * Then the system displays the totals per tax type (GST/HST/PST)
* Scenario 3: Locked Fee Report
   * Given a guest has entered their card details and then canceled their stay
   * When I open the Locked Fee report
   * Then I can release the held amount immediately

# Business Rules

* Amounts are presented in CAD.
* Dates and times are presented in the property's local timezone (stored in UTC).
* Night Audit report (per property and business date): run status and start/end times, number of In-House reservations charged, total room revenue, taxes per type, total posted, a detail line per reservation (confirmation code, room, room type, guest, nightly rate, tax type and amount, total posted) and an exceptions list of reservations that could not be charged.
* Tax Summary report (per property and period): one line per tax type and rate (taxable amount, tax collected, number of transactions), with a breakdown by source (room charges, fines, fees) and by month for periods longer than one month.
* Locked Fee report (General Manager only, per property): every canceled reservation that still has money held on the guest's card (cancellation date, confirmation code, guest, held amount, held since, origin of the hold, cancellation fee owed, status and who released it and when), with a "Release now" action that releases the hold immediately through the payment gateway and records it in the audit log.
* All reports show a header (property, report name, business date or period, generated at and generated by), summary totals at the top and a detail table that can be sorted and filtered.
* All reports can be exported as PDF and CSV.
