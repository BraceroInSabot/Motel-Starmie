[US-22] Night Audit, Tax Summary and Held Funds Reports

# User Story

As a General Manager, I want Night Audit reports, tax summaries and a Held Funds report, So that I can monitor financial results, meet tax obligations and return held money to guests when necessary.

# Acceptance Criteria

* Scenario 1: Night Audit report
   * Given the Night Audit ran for a business day
   * When I open the Night Audit report for that day
   * Then the system displays the charges and taxes posted in that run and the reservations that could not be charged
* Scenario 2: Tax summary
   * Given there are taxes posted in a period
   * When I generate the tax summary for that period
   * Then the system displays the totals per tax (HST, Marketing Levy) and rate
* Scenario 3: Held Funds report
   * Given a guest has checked out, and the release of their deposit hold failed and is still being retried
   * When I open the Held Funds report and press "Release now"
   * Then the system releases the hold through Stripe immediately and records the action in the audit log
* Scenario 4: Failed refund
   * Given a refund to a guest failed at Stripe
   * When I open the Held Funds report and press "Retry refund"
   * Then the system sends the refund again through Stripe and records the action in the audit log
* Scenario 5: Export
   * Given I am viewing a report
   * When I export it as PDF or CSV
   * Then the system downloads the report with the same content and header

# Business Rules

* Amounts are presented in CAD.
* Dates and times are presented in the property's local timezone (stored in UTC).
* Reports are generated for one property at a time. The General Manager switches between properties. There is no combined report in V1.
* Night Audit report (per property and business date): run status and start/end times, number of In-House reservations charged, total room revenue, taxes per type, total posted, a detail line per reservation (confirmation code, room, room type, guest, nightly rate, tax type and amount, total posted) and an exceptions list of reservations that could not be charged.
* Tax Summary report (per property and period): one line per tax (HST and Marketing Levy) and rate, with the taxable amount, the tax collected and the number of transactions, and a breakdown by month for periods longer than one month. Fines and fees are not taxed (see US-04).
* Held Funds report (General Manager only, per property): every reservation that is no longer staying (Checked-Out, Canceled or No-Show) and still has money held or owed back to the guest:
   * a deposit hold that has not been released yet (e.g., the release failed at check-out and is still being retried, see US-17);
   * a refund that failed at Stripe.
* For each line, the Held Funds report shows: the confirmation code, the guest, the reservation status, the amount, the type (deposit hold or refund), since when the money has been held or owed, the status, and who acted on it and when.
* The Held Funds report has a "Release now" action for deposit holds and a "Retry refund" action for failed refunds. Both act immediately through Stripe and are recorded in the audit log.
* All reports show a header (property, report name, business date or period, generated at and generated by), summary totals at the top and a detail table that can be sorted and filtered.
* All reports can be exported as PDF and CSV. PDFs are generated with WeasyPrint from HTML templates, which are also used for the invoice (see US-17).
